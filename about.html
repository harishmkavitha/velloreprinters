About us

Fourteen years of ink, paper and kept promises

Vellore Printers is a family-run printing company. We print the unglamorous things that make a business look serious — and we do it when we said we would.

Our story

The press began in 2011 with one single-colour machine and a simple rule: the customer hears the truth the same day we do. No job delayed quietly because nobody wanted to make a phone call.

That rule survived a move to a bigger unit, three new presses and a team of eighteen. Today the studio and the press floor run side by side for schools, hospitals, builders, restaurants and local brands across Vellore district.

We are not the largest printer around. We are the one that picks up the phone at 7 pm when your event is tomorrow morning.

What we hold to

Four rules we don’t bend

Quality we can defend

If a sheet leaves the floor with a scuff, a mis-register or a shade drift, it is reprinted at our cost. No debate.

Honest estimating

We will tell you when 130 GSM does the same job as 170 GSM, even though the cheaper quote earns us less.

Lower-waste printing

Soy-based inks, chemistry-free plates and paper offcuts routed to a recycler every fortnight.

Everything in-house

Design, pre-press, offset, digital, wide-format, lamination, die-cutting and binding under one roof.

How we grew

From one machine to a full floor

  • 2011Opened with one single-colour offset machine and three people in a small unit off Officers Line.
  • 2015Added a four-colour offset press and moved into our current larger unit near the New Bus Stand.
  • 2018Launched the in-house design studio and Tamil typesetting desk.
  • 2021Installed wide-format and UV printers for signage, gifting and rigid media.
  • 2024Commissioned an Ultra HD digital press for short-run 4K work and same-day cards.
  • 2026Serving 3,200+ businesses across Vellore district with a 98% on-time dispatch record.
