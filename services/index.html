How an order runs

Four steps, no surprises

01

Brief & design

Send artwork or a rough idea. Our studio prepares a print-ready layout and shares a digital proof.

02

Proof & approval

You check colour, spelling and dimensions. Nothing runs until you approve in writing.

03

Press & finishing

Plates or digital output, colour-bar sampling, then lamination, foiling, die-cutting or binding.

04

Count & deliver

Quantity verified twice, shrink-wrapped and delivered free across Vellore town.
