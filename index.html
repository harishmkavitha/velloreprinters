Why Vellore Printers

What makes our printing different?

Ultra HD output

2400 dpi plates and inline colour control give clean gradients, hairline rules and true skin tones on every stock.

In-house studio

Designers on staff for logo, layout and Tamil–English typesetting before anything reaches the press.

Imported papers

Bond, textured, FBB, kraft and triplex boards stocked — you are never forced into a compromise.

24-hour express

Standard cards and flyers dispatched the next working day across Vellore and nearby towns.

Proof before print

A digital proof on every job and a wet proof on large runs — you sign off, then we run.

Free local delivery

No delivery charge inside Vellore town, Katpadi, Sathuvachari, Gandhi Nagar and Bagayam.

Inside the press floor

From file to finished box, under one roof

Pre-press check, colour profiling, plate output, press run, lamination, die-cutting, quality sampling and packing — all of it happens in our unit. Nothing is sub-contracted without telling you first.

  • Pre-press artwork check within 2 hours of file upload
  • Colour-bar sampling every 500 sheets
  • Final count verified twice before dispatch
